01 / About

About the game

You play as WHOAMI, an anonymous hacker who takes jobs on closed forums. People come to you with cold cases the police gave up on years ago. You get databases, email, phone records, maps, street cameras and social media. No hints, no "find" buttons.

Fake desktop OS

Apps open as movable, overlapping windows: database, mail, map, cameras, terminal, and more.

Fully manual investigation

Every search, every login, every password is on you. How-tos live in an in-game handbook, not in tooltips.

A living database

Not just suspects. Every resident of the city can be looked up, and some of them know more than they think.

Fictional cases, real places

Real US cities and real platforms like Instagram, Google, Strava and Steam. Fictional people and companies.

Twisty cases

Several suspects with motives, red herrings that make sense in the end, evidence spread across many apps.

Graded reports

File a report: who, where, when, how, and up to five pieces of evidence. Get a score, a grade from A+ to F, and one of several endings.
